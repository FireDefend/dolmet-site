随口译

面对面说话，边说边译，不联网

随口译是一款完全离线的 iPhone 翻译应用：把手机放在两个人中间，谁说话都不用碰手机，它自动判断说的是哪种语言，边说边把译文显示给对方；打字翻译则用手机能装下的高质量翻译模型，长句和专业表达也能译好。模型下载完成后，全部功能在飞行模式下可用。

常见问题

  • 第一次打开要下载什么？ 标准翻译模型（约 1.1 GB）和 iOS 系统的语音识别资源。建议在 Wi-Fi 下完成；之后不再联网。
  • 高级模型是什么？ 更大的文字翻译模型（约 4.6 GB），翻译质量更高，需要 12 GB 内存的 iPhone 机型（如 iPhone 17 Pro）。对话模式始终使用标准模型。
  • 对话模式支持哪些语言？ 中文、粤语、日语、韩语 与 英语、德语、西班牙语、法语、葡萄牙语、意大利语 之间任意两种不同文字体系的组合。
  • 语音识别资源显示"不支持"？ 对话模式需要 iOS 26 及支持系统实时语音识别的机型；在"模型"页可添加和下载其他语言的识别资源。
  • 翻错了怎么办？ 在对话页或文字页菜单里点"反馈问题"，勾选要附带的内容后发送给开发者。内容和处理方式见隐私政策。

Dolmet

Face-to-face talk, no internet

Dolmet is an iPhone translator that works entirely offline. Put the phone between two people: nobody has to touch it, it detects which language is being spoken and shows the translation to the other person while you are still talking. For typed text it uses the best translation model your phone can hold, so long sentences and technical wording come out right. Once the models are downloaded, everything works in Airplane Mode.

FAQ

  • What is downloaded on first launch? The Standard translation model (about 1.1 GB) and the iOS speech recognition assets. Do it on Wi-Fi; after that the app never needs the network.
  • What is the Advanced model? A larger text translation model (about 4.6 GB) with higher quality. It needs an iPhone with 12 GB of memory (such as iPhone 17 Pro). Conversation mode always uses the Standard model.
  • Which languages does Conversation mode support? Any pair of different writing systems among Chinese, Cantonese, Japanese, Korean and English, German, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Italian.
  • Speech assets show "Not supported"? Conversation mode needs iOS 26 and a device that supports the system's live speech recognition. Other languages can be added and downloaded on the Models tab.
  • A translation was wrong? Choose "Report a problem" from the Conversation or Text menu, tick what to include and send it to the developer. See the privacy policy for what is sent and how it is handled.
